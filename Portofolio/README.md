### BSc Thesis: 
* My thesis focused on evaluating the design and performance of the underground support system for Tunnel 3 in Sigli, Banda Aceh. I characterized the rock mass using available geological and geotechnical datasets and translated the results into parameters for finite element modeling (RS2). The analysis evaluated the deformation of the surrounding rock mass and the interaction between the rock mass and support system following tunnel excavation.

### Deep Geology Waste Repository: 
* This project was created as a final assignment for the Energy and Environment Geotechnic course. The project aimed to assess the behavior of underground excavations for a deep geological repository for nuclear waste. The construction of the repository alters the surrounding stress field, while heat generated by the nuclear waste affects the temperature and thermal stresses within the surrounding rock. Using a finite element model in PLAXIS, I developed a coupled model to analyze the resulting changes in stress and deformation caused by the construction and operation of the deep geological repository.

### MSc Thesis:
* My master`s thesis focused on estimating the slip deficit on the East Anatolia Fault Zone using data assimilation combined with a half-space elastic model by Savage and Buford (1970) with GNSS dataset from Kurt et al(2022). The estimated slip deficit was then used to gain insight into how strain accumulates and is distributed along the fault zone. The results can contribute to a better understanding of the region’s seismic hazard and support local authorities and communities in assessing and mitigating potential earthquake risks and future disasters.
